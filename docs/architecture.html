Pitchlens

How Pitchlens works

A desktop app that turns a sales call recording into a coaching report, using two small AI models on your Mac.

● 100% on-device Zero network egress Apple Silicon GPU (Metal) Electron · llama.cpp · ffmpeg

The pipeline — from recording to report

1

Upload

Rep drops in a call recording (MP3, WAV, M4A…) or picks a sample.

drag & drop
2

Prepare audio

Converted to 16 kHz mono and cut into ~25 s pieces at natural pauses.

ffmpeg
3

Transcribe

Each piece becomes text. Lines appear in the app live as they finish.

Qwen3-ASR 0.6B
4

Analyze

The language model reads the transcript like a sales coach and fills in the report.

Qwen3.5 2B
5

Report

Shown as cards, saved to history, exported as PDF or copied.

Privacy by design

  • Model servers are offline and listen only on 127.0.0.1
  • The window blocks every non-local request
  • Audio, transcripts and reports stay on the Mac

What the sales rep gets

  • Summary, call type and buyer sentiment
  • Mistakes with evidence and a better way to say it
  • Conversion probability (70% model + 30% skill rubric)
  • Objections raised and a suggested response
  • Skill scores: rapport, discovery, pitch, objections, closing
  • Suggestions for this call, recommendations to practise
  • Next steps with the prospect
  • Timestamped transcript with click-to-play audio
